01

The datum

One vertical hairline at column 5 of 16, running the full height of every page, uninterrupted, behind all content.

Left of it is claim — what we say. Right of it is evidence — what backs it. It never moves, never breaks, and appears on every page including 404. Once it exists, layout decisions stop being arbitrary, which is the entire reason an architect draws a datum.

Its position is derived, not typed. With 16 columns of width c and gutter g across a content track of width W, the left edge of column 5 is 4(c + g), and c = (W − 15g) / 16. Change the gutter token and the datum follows; nobody re-measures anything.

Below lg there are not 16 columns. The datum then holds its proportion of the content measure rather than snapping to a column that does not exist.

Claim / evidence, 9 : 7

This block sits in the evidence columns. Every content section and every product screen splits 9 / 7 across the page — never 50/50, because symmetry is passive and kills hierarchy. Visitors are trained by the layout before they ever reach the product.

03

The spacing scale

A closed set. No other spacing value exists — that is a LAW, and tools/lint-tokens.py fails the build on a literal that is not in it. Baseline grid 8px; type sits on a 4px sub-grid.

04

The type scale

Two ratios, because editorial and interface have different jobs: Perfect Fourth 1.333 anchored at 18px for editorial, Minor Third 1.200 anchored at 15px for interface. Tracking is not chosen by eye — it comes off a curve, −0.0165 + 0.29/size, and every value below is that formula evaluated and rounded.

Rendering in fallback. Redaction, Apfel Grotezk and Sligoil are not in this repository. The sizes, leadings, tracking and measure below are real and judgeable; the letterforms are stand-ins. See fonts/README.md.

The two gates that run on every change

Contrast is asserted, not assumed. Twenty pairs, each against the worst ground its token may legally land on, computed from tokens.css through OKLCH → Oklab → sRGB rather than read off a table someone typed.

The token lint enforces the two rules that keep a system from dissolving: no colour literal outside tokens.css, and no spacing, duration or curve outside its closed set.

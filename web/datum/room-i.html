What Room I is doing

The mirror is not showing you the plate. It is showing you a second plate, and two of its three values are wrong.

In the built version the reflection is a render target pointed at a second scene, and the false plate tracks the pointer at 0.84× the real one — it almost follows. Here it is two elements in the DOM with different text, mirrored and sheared by 0.9° at the current decohere value of 0.34. Different mechanism, identical read, and the read is what FE-1's gate is for: if the composition does not hold in CSS it will not be rescued by a shader in FE-5.

Every gradient on this page belongs to one lamp, standing high and to the left, out of frame. That is law L-5, and the test for it is whether a photographer could stand in this room and take this picture.

Type substitution notice. Redaction, Apfel Grotezk and Sligoil are not in this repository — see web/datum/fonts/README.md. Everything above is rendering in its fallback stack, so the degradation axis (§5.2) currently carries no visual difference between a verified and an unverified claim. The scale, the tracking curve and the layout are real; the faces are not yet. FE-1's gate report records this as open.

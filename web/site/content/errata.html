Errata

Defects found in this product, published by us, before anyone asked. A company selling a verification layer that does not publish its own defects is asking for a trust it will not extend.

Fixed

G-1 — evidence boxes landed wrong on rotated pages

The reviewer console projected a PDF box onto the rendered page by multiplying by the zoom. That is correct only for a page with no rotation and no cropbox offset — which every document in our corpus happens to be. On a page rotated 90°, every evidence box landed on a different part of the page.

It could not fire on the corpus we had, and was certain to fire on the corpus we had committed to: rotated pages are how datasheets carry fold-out tables, and fold-outs are named in our own frozen test split. Fixed by deleting the arithmetic and importing the single canonical implementation — then tested by rendering the page and looking at the pixels underneath the box, which is the only check that can tell "correct" from "correct by coincidence on this corpus".

G-2 — scans carrying an OCR layer were treated as evidence

A scanned document with an OCR text layer reported 161,731 words and nothing declined it. Those words are a model's reading of pixels, not the document's own text, so a citation into them quotes something the document does not say — and the evidence box has nothing true to point at.

Fixed by measuring image area per page and declining with a reason that names what actually happened. This lowers our measured coverage. It was never grounding; it was grounding-shaped, and the lower number is the honest one.

O-11 — a refusal named a decision the reviewer did not make

A reviewer who rejected a redline and kept their own catalog value was told their acceptance had been refused. It survived because the test asserted that an error was raised, never what the error said.

Open

One page fails our own projection gate

A born-digital page in the ABB S200 M UC datasheet measures 54% agreement against a 60% bar. Two candidate fixes are named; neither is tested. The bar has not been moved.

A measurement may be sitting at the edge of its own search window

One document's best-fit offset lands exactly on the boundary of the range we search, which means the true value may lie outside it. Unexplained. Recorded rather than rounded off.

A threshold was moved to make a measurement pass

A contrast threshold was changed from 1.5 to 1.25 after it rejected 80% of otherwise good words. It was the right change for the right reason, and it is also exactly the shape of the wrong one. It is listed here because it needs a reviewer who is not its author.

The typography this design specifies is not installed

Three type families are specified and absent, so every page you are reading renders in a fallback face. The design's most distinctive decision — type that degrades as its source becomes less certain — has never been seen by anyone, including us.

This list is not complete in the sense of "everything that is wrong". It is complete in the sense of "everything we have found and not yet fixed". The difference matters, and claiming the first would be the same category of error as everything above.

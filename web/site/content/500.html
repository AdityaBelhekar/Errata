500

Something failed.

This page exists. We failed to produce it. That is a fault on our side, not a wrong address and not a missing record.

The distinction is worth the extra page. A 404 means there is nothing here; this means there is something here and you have not been shown it. Anything you were part-way through has not been recorded as a decision — the ledger takes a decision or it does not, and a failure in between leaves nothing half-written.

Retrying is safe. If it fails again, the console prints one line per request to its own terminal, and that line is more useful to us than any description of what the screen did.
